# F01 — Design System

The visual language and brand assets for Coffeetosh. This feature owns the color system, typography direction, geometry, logo states, and marketing asset requirements used by the macOS app and website.

## What We Build

- Warm Amber and Espresso Dark visual tokens.
- Light-theme fallback tokens.
- Logo asset states and their usage rules.
- Shared spacing, corner-radius, button, and animation conventions.
- Production-ready app and marketing assets.
